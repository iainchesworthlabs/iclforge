LVGL
----

LVGL (https://lvgl.io), the graphics library, is compiled into the image of
Hearth's ESP32-P4 sink with a screen (the firmware this release carries for
that board), which draws its lyric screen with it. It is not part of Hearth's
desktop programs. LVGL is offered under the MIT licence, which follows as LVGL
distributes it. The third-party parts of LVGL that are compiled into that
image (stb_truetype, and the Montserrat font) are covered with the
firmware's components, below.
