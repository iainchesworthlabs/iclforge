    Roadmap¶

    Candidate and in-flight work only. This is not a commitment.

    For what already ships, see CHANGELOG.md and each product's index page. For the library's capability record, see docs/library/capabilities.md. Detailed design lives in planning/ and product design/ records — linked below, not copied here.

    Last reviewed: 2026-10-13; the TrueHD, AC-4 content signing and Common Encryption rows, 2026-10-29. Reconciliation source: planning/roadmap-inventory.md.

    How to read this¶

    Status Meaning
    In progress Active development on main, or on a named branch named below
    Partial Some of the scope is shipped; the row splits what is done from what is not
    Proposed Strong candidate or agreed direction; not started, or study only
    Blocked Wanted but waiting on an external dependency
    Out of scope Deliberately not doing (with reason)

    Sizes, where they still matter, are rough: S (an afternoon), M (a day or two), L (a focused week), XL (several PRs).

    Library — AC-4 decode and encode (Partial)¶

    The decoder, the encoder, the IEC 61937 carriage, the applications, the decoder on the ESP32 parts and AC-4 in a Sendspin group on the ESP32 sinks are built, and the tree has the new names. What ships is in CHANGELOG.md and AC-4 in the library; this table keeps what is unfinished.

    Layer State
    Inspector, decoder and encoder (libs/ac4) Shipped (phases D1 to D10 and E1 to E10 of planning/ac4.md). Decode to PCM: mono, stereo, 3.0, 5.X and 7.X in every codec mode Part 1 gives them (SIMPLE, ASPX and the A-CPL modes), the immersive element of 7.0.4 and 7.1.4 in full and core decoding, the 22.2 element in full decoding as coded (24 channels, checked on constructed streams alone), A-JOC and direct-coded objects with their metadata, streams of several presentations, every frame rate (the efficient high frame rate mode included), the speech spectral frontend, the output level, DRC, dialogue enhancement, the downmix and concealment. Encode: mono, stereo, 5.0 and 5.1 in the SIMPLE, ASPX and A-CPL modes, 5.0.4 and 5.1.4 in the immersive element, several substreams and the presentations of Part 2 Table 53, every frame rate, constant, average and variable rates, I-frames and the metadata, and, behind experimental.objects, A-JOC and direct-coded objects. The three libraries are installed and exported (AC-4 in the library, encoding a stream, encoding objects)
    What they refuse, and what is experimental The decoder refuses, by name, core decoding of 22.2 and its rendering to any layout but as coded (Part 2 gives it neither), and, at 96 or 192 kHz, everything but the SIMPLE codec mode (A-SPX and A-CPL, the speech spectral frontend, the immersive and 22.2 elements, objects, the mixing of a presentation's substreams, dialogue enhancement and DRC's compression curve; the output level and the downmix apply). The speech spectral frontend, the efficient high frame rate mode, the 9.X.4 modes and the SIMPLE mode at 96 and 192 kHz are decoded from the text alone, no stream from outside the project using any of them. The encoder writes as experimental options what DEE's streams do not contain and no reader outside the project has read from it: 7.0 and 7.1 in the 7.X element, 3.0, the 5.X element's other coding configurations, ASPX_ACPL_1 and A-CPL in stereo, balance coding, VARVAR framing and frequency interleaving in A-SPX, 7.0.4 and 7.1.4 with the back pair, A-JCC, objects, spectral noise fill, the efficient high frame rate mode (at a constant rate), 9.X.4 and 22.2
    IEC 61937 carriage (iclforge::containers::iec61937, PassthroughSink, Hearth's extension role) Built (phase D11) — the four IEC 61937-14 burst types at every frame rate, read back unchanged; passthrough on ALSA and Android, the platforms whose APIs can send AC-4; the extension role's AC-4 data type, decoded by the test sink and by an ESP32-P4 or ESP32-S3 sink built with the decoder; no receiver found accepts AC-4
    Applications (forge, Hearth, Forge GUI, bindings) Shipped (phases I1 to I5b). forge reads and writes AC-4 in decode, ac4-encode, transcode, record, live, monitor, play, qc, levels, loudness, spdif, probe, mp4, ts, fmp4, atmos-encode, atmos-adm and atmos-iab; mkv refuses it, since Matroska registers no codec ID. Hearth's desktop player plays channel-based, immersive and object AC-4, decoded for every output and sent as bursts to network sinks that list it, with the Decoder page's AC-4 controls and the Media page's AC-4 information. The Forge GUI encodes AC-4 from a file (mono to 5.1, or objects, to a raw stream or an MP4 file), and its QC, player, Open stream and object pages read it. The C API and the Python, Rust and WebAssembly bindings decode and encode it, objects included. Android's CMake wrapper builds the AC-4 library; the Shield app does not link it. Not done: an AC-4 live session in the GUI; Pro Logic II from Hearth's Decoder page; a WebAssembly demo page for the AC-4 module; the encoder's substreams, presentations, loudness, DRC, downmix and dialogue groups in the bindings; a gap of about 27 ms between two AC-4 items in a Hearth queue, since the decoder's delay is not trimmed at an item's start
    ESP32 (float on the P4 and the S3; fixed point on the C6 and the C3) Built (plan phases D14a to D14i, and I6). The ESP-IDF component builds the decoder behind CONFIG_ICLFORGE_AC4, in float or in fixed point by target, as AC-3 and E-AC-3 are, and the PCM of the pinned fixtures is the host's on each part. An ESP32-P4 at 360 MHz with Wi-Fi up keeps up at 2.0 in every mode and frame rate and at 5.1 in all four codec modes (0.42 to 0.63 of a frame), and takes 5.1.4 at 0.94 to 1.15 (ESP32-P4). An ESP32-S3 keeps up at 2.0 (0.40 in SIMPLE mode, 0.50 with A-SPX) and through the frame-rate converter, and takes 5.1 in SIMPLE mode at 0.99 (ESP32-S3). The fixed-point tier gives one hash on the host, the Cortex-M3 leg and RV32IMC; a 2.0 decode needs 286,365 bytes, which a C6 does not have beside Wi-Fi, so a C6 refuses the play and its sink takes AC-4 programmes from Hearth as PCM (ESP32-C6). hearth_sink decodes AC-4 sent in a Sendspin group (I6): a P4 lists ac4 at 6 channels, an S3 with the 64 KB data cache at 2 channels and 25 frames a second, neither with objects, and a server decodes what is past those and sends PCM. Not done: the decoder in the published firmware images; 5.1.4 in real time on the P4 with A-SPX; 5.1 with A-SPX or A-CPL on the S3; a presentation's objects decoded on a board

    Plan: planning/ac4.md. Its first phase (G0) made the DEE reference streams the others need, and G1 made the golden masters for the phases after it; the local DEE licence ends on 2026-11-06 and will not be renewed, so a stream DEE has not been asked for cannot be made after that date.

    Library — TrueHD module (was IM5, Partial)¶

    libs/truehd (iclforge::truehd, ICLFORGE_BUILD_TRUEHD, on by default) is built and checked against FFmpeg. It is written from the owner's TrueHD (MLP) Reference Specification, which is not in the tree, and every place that specification and the streams disagree is an entry or an open question in libs/truehd/ERRATA.md. The plan is planning/truehd.md; what ships is in CHANGELOG.md and TrueHD and MLP in the library. This table keeps what is unfinished. The branch feature/truehd-atmos-support is superseded: no TrueHD decoder reads its block layout and it carries non-redistributable PDFs, so nothing is taken from it.

    Layer State
    Decoder and encoder (libs/truehd) Built (phases T1 and T2a of the plan). The decoder reads TrueHD and MLP: substreams 0 to 2 and a generic 16-channel substream 3, 44.1 to 192 kHz, 16 and 24 bits, bit-exact with FFmpeg's strict decode on its 25 committed fixtures; an IIR filter, LSB bypass, noise generators 0 and 1, a negative output shift, several matrices, the block data check, terminators and extra data are checked against 29 hand-built streams, of which FFmpeg's decoder reads 19. The encoder writes TrueHD, 1 to 8 channels of 16, 20 or 24 bits, and FFmpeg's strict decode returns every sample in 43 cases and a fuzzer; its streams are 0.85 to 0.96 of FFmpeg's encoder's size at 44.1 to 96 kHz, larger at 192 kHz, and 0.966 of DEE's on the 5.1 music (measured). Not done: heavy DRC (the streams do not show where its bits sit), and in the encoder an IIR filter, LSB bypass, a negative output shift, heavy DRC, noise and MLP; nothing outside this library reads the encoder's 2 and 6 channel downmix presentations
    Containers and carriage (libs/containers) Built (phase T2c1). MP4 (mlpa, dmlp), Matroska (A_TRUEHD), MPEG-TS (stream_type 0x83, Blu-ray profile) and MAT (IEC 61937 data type 22), in both directions, bit-exact with FFmpeg's muxers. Not done: every layout has one oracle, FFmpeg, and FFmpeg's spdif demuxer reads no MAT stream, so reading MAT has none; nothing has been played into a receiver or a disc player; an M2TS writer and the TrueHD-plus-AC-3-core arrangement of a disc (libs/containers/ERRATA.md)
    Applications (forge) Built (phase T2c2). decode, probe, levels, loudness, mkv, mp4, ts, spdif, unspdif, demux, remux and truehd-encode; the codec matrix, the gold-reference gate and the FFmpeg Validate job hold them to FFmpeg's strict decode. Not done: transcode, qc, fmp4, monitor and play refuse a TrueHD input; the Forge GUI does not link the library (Crucible links it for one pinned mode); the project file does not name truehd-encode; truehd-encode refuses an .mlp name
    C API and bindings The C API (iclforge_truehd_*, decode and encode) is Built. Not done: the Python, JavaScript (WebAssembly) and Rust bindings (was UX10); each is a follow-up on the C API alone
    Atmos substream and object audio metadata (phase T2b) Blocked on the owner's answers about the evolution payload (libs/truehd/ERRATA.md, open questions 1 and 2, and 22 to 39 for the v3.6 syntax). The library reads the extra data's evolution frame into plain structures, provisionally, and forge probe reports it (detail=format is the check to run on a disc's stream); nothing decodes the objects and no Atmos stream is written (the plan).
    Content signing for TrueHD Not built. The owner's specification describes the evolution payload as the container AC-4's content signature uses, but lists its field IDs and the digest scope as unconfirmed (§16.3.3). iclforge::base::crypto is codec-neutral and would carry it; see the AC-4 content signing row below and Signing
    Scoring against Dolby Encoding Engine tools/checks/score_truehd_decode.py has been run on streams this tree made and not on DEE's. DEE's licence ends on 2026-11-06 and is not renewed; the set of known-source TrueHD streams (2, 6 and 8 channels, 48 and 96 kHz, 16 and 24 bits, several presentations) is made by tools/generators/gen_dee_gold.py and stays on the owner's machine

    Parity with E-AC-3 in every product and tool is the next section.

    TrueHD and E-AC-3 as equals (Proposed)¶

    The owner's direction is that TrueHD and E-AC-3 (DD+) are equal first-class citizens: where one has a row, the other has a row with its honest status. Round 1 of the audit compares each area with E-AC-3 and writes a planning document per area; this section links each when it lands and takes its sizes (the scale above) from it. No implementation below is started.

    Forge GUI Nothing: no page opens, plays, inspects, checks or encodes TrueHD An Open stream, QC, player and encode route for TrueHD Parity audit

The device-free tests unwrap the bursts and decode them with this library to the rounded input, sample for sample, and FFmpeg's strict decode agrees. The output decision lists each platform's run status and gives the fallback chain with no transcode; the player does not send MAT yet. Not done: MAT to a receiver (a run on any platform, and the player's call site), a track picker and a window control for the track preference, dialogue normalisation and range compression Parity audit
    Hearth for Android Not linked; passes AC-3 and E-AC-3 to the receiver Pass-through of the coded stream where the device takes it Parity audit
    ESP32 sinks The decoder is measured on the C6 and the P4 by firmware/truehd-probe (bit-exact on all eight fixtures; 158 KB of heap and 60 KB of stack; real-time factor 0.13 on the P4 and 0.34 on the C6 for 2.0 at 48 kHz, 0.32 and 0.80 for 5.1). Not linked into a sink; the S3 not measured The S3 figure (its board needs a cable replug), the decoder's footprint, a CONFIG_ switch in the component, then a sink that plays it Parity audit, measured
    Library bindings The C API only Python, JavaScript (WebAssembly) and Rust Not done in T2c; parity audit
    Performance and platform kernels TrueHD is in the real-time guards, the speed, memory and kernel benchmarks and the trend pages, with host instruction ceilings and an AVX2 kernel for the encoder's bit count; it is not in the Cortex-M3 and ESP32 probes, the footprint report or the per-platform pages Instruction counts and image sizes on the small parts, platform pages with measured numbers, and a minimum-footprint profile The performance and platforms audit

    Content signing (TrueHD). Two accounts of the evolution frame's check exist and neither has been checked against a stream. An earlier note in this project, taken from the superseded branch, described a truncated HMAC-SHA-256 over the access unit and the evolution frame. The owner's specification (v3.4 and later) describes the payload as the same ContentSignature container, in Thrift and under the same pace-ccjf1 scheme, as AC-4's content signing. The specification is the later source and the one the code follows, so any TrueHD verification would sit on iclforge::base::crypto, not on iclforge::ac3::signing's EMDF path. See Object signing.

    Out of scope¶

    • Forging Dolby's authenticity tag — see docs/concepts/object-signing.md. The signer ships; the key is the operator's. Multi-key verify and the licensed soft-gate use operator-provisioned keys only — they do not recover or invent decoder secrets.
    • AC-3 VBR — structurally impossible; frame size indexes a fixed table.
    • Room correction and equalisation — covered by Cavern. Hearth renders to the speakers and manages them (per-output trim and delay, a bass-management crossover) and does no measurement, equalisation or filtering beyond the crossover. A headphone/binaural preview for the WASM demo stays off unless that boundary is redrawn on purpose.
    • A DAMF reader — no public specification; IM1 / ADM BWF is the replacement.
    • TrueHD interoperability by black-box analysis of Dolby streams — not until the clean-room rule explicitly allows it.
    • An external oracle for fscod2 audio — FFmpeg and Dolby Reference Player refuse it.
    • Perfect separation of co-directional objects — property of parametric object coding.
    • Enabling PipeWire iec958Codecs on the user's behalf — session-manager policy; documented instead.
    • HOA, Matrix and Binaural ADM pack types in the Atmos bridge — refused with kUnsupportedType: AtmosEncoder takes positioned mono objects, and none of the three is one. The parser reads all three, a Matrix block's coefficients and its pack's references included (from the axml text, since libadm skips Matrix blocks), and the writer writes them; only the bridge refuses.
    • AC-4 in Matroska — Matroska registers no codec ID for AC-4, so forge mkv refuses it; MP4, CMAF and MPEG-TS carry it.
    • AC-4 beyond the plan's scope — transcoding between AC-4 and AC-3 or E-AC-3 without decoding (forge transcode goes through PCM); rendering objects inside the decoder, headphone virtualisation and head tracking; presentations spread over several elementary streams; protected (encrypted) tracks (an MP4 with one is refused, naming its scheme, original format and default KID; nothing decrypts it); MPEG-TS under ATSC A/342-2's profile (DVB's is carried); the speech spectral frontend, 96 and 192 kHz, A-SPX time-interleaved waveform coding and bitstream versions 0 and 1 in the encoder (accepted gaps, each with its reason in the development status page); writing immersive stereo (presentation_version 2); the encoder on any ESP32; and AC-4 in Crucible, which captures applications into Atmos objects for receivers when no receiver accepts AC-4. The decoder's speech frontend, 9.X.4 modes and 96 and 192 kHz decoding wait for a stream that uses them. See Deliberately not in scope.
    • APT/DNF repositories and Docker images — not planned; see docs/releasing.md.
    • ESP32-P4 as a replacement for the S3 Wi-Fi Sendspin sink — closed 2026-09-08 (no on-die radio; no float PIE win; S3 probe already real-time). Complementary P4 “best” module (Ethernet / hosted C6, dual ES9080 @ 32-bit) is partly built: the probe and the hosted-Wi-Fi sink exist, TDM and the ES9080 pair do not — see planning/esp32-sink-tiers.md and esp32-c3.md.
    • Per-channel and per-block SNR offsets — tried and declined (EQ2); reference encoders agree with shipped behaviour.
    • Multi-service (multi-PID) MPEG-TS authoring — one PMT with a main-service PID plus associated-service PIDs, built in one invocation. iclforge::containers::mpegts::mux stays a single-elementary-stream muxer (its own header comment calls a general multiplexer out of scope); mainid/asvc describe links an operator authors across separately-muxed files, not a multiplex this tool builds for them.
