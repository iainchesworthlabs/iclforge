**Encoding E-AC-3 and Atmos from the command line takes less time.** `forge eac3-encode` of 100 s of 5.1 at 448 kbit/s went from 2.086 s to 1.703 s (about 18 % less) and at 192 kbit/s from 1.720 s to 1.460 s (about 15 % less); `forge atmos-encode` of 25 s at 768 kbit/s went from 0.709 s to 0.594 s (about 16 % less). The encoder's exponent-run planner, which decides where each channel refreshes its exponent sets and with which strategy, now skips candidate runs that provably cannot win and builds only the exponents the coded bins read. The encoded bytes are the same as before. Streams coded with the adaptive hybrid transform gain little, because they do not use this planner.
