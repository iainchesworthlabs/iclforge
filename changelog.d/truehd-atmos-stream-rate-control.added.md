**TrueHD streams are held to a bitrate, and `forge probe detail=rate` reports how well.** The encoder's arrival model (TH spec 3.4 and 18: a unit may start when its slot opens and the unit before it has arrived, and must have arrived within 75 ms of its slot) is now a library type, `iclforge::truehd::RateModel`, and it runs twice, at the declared peak rate and at a bitrate the caller chooses (`truehd-bitrate=<kbit/s>` on `truehd-encode` and `truehd-atmos`, `EncoderConfig::rate_control`). The model used to keep the lag of a late unit for the rest of the stream, so one burst of loud audio could end a long stream; a backlog now drains when later units arrive in less than their own duration. `forge probe FILE detail=rate` (and `json=1`) prints, for each restart interval, its bitrate, the rate of its largest unit and the least headroom before a unit would be late, in whole kbit/s and milliseconds with three decimals, so that the output is the same on every run; `truehd-bitrate=` on a probe runs the model at that rate instead of the declared one. `truehd-atmos truehd-rate-mode=shape` lets the object channels of substream 3 give up low bits, one at a time, in the restart intervals that do not fit (`truehd-shape-bits=`, 8 by default): the 7.1 bed is never touched, the objects of such an interval are the input's rounded to a multiple of 2^k, and the status line says how many intervals it happened in. It is off by default and PROVISIONAL (ERRATA question 71): whether a lossy object substream is acceptable is the owner's decision.
