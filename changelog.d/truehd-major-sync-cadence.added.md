**A TrueHD stream can carry a major sync less often than it restarts.** `truehd-major-sync=<access units>` on `truehd-encode` and `truehd-atmos` (`EncoderConfig::major_sync_interval_units`) puts the major sync at every N-th access unit, a multiple of `truehd-restart=`; the restart intervals between two major syncs open with a restart header in every substream and no major sync. The default, 0, is a major sync at every restart header, which is what the encoder always wrote. A decoder that joins the stream partway waits for the next major sync. The container's rules (restart cadence, directory entries, parity and CRC bytes, extra data framing) and the 1536-sample placement of the object audio metadata are now `iclforge/truehd/encoder/layout.hpp`, with `audit_layout()` to hold a stream to them; `truehd-atmos` writes the metadata exactly as it did.
