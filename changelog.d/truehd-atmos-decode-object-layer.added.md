**`forge decode` reads the object layer of a TrueHD Atmos stream (WP1 steps 2 and 3; provisional).** With `truehd-presentation=all`, `objects_dir` writes each channel of substream 3 as a mono WAV and `adm_out` an ADM master (beds as DirectSpeakers, dynamic objects positioned by the update blocks of the OAMD payloads in the evolution frames, timed from the access unit that carried them); `truehd-render=<layout>` places the objects on a speaker layout with `libs/render` and writes the feeds as the WAV; `adm_out` with another presentation is that presentation's bed alone. The channel-to-object mapping, the timing and the cadence are provisional (ERRATA entry 61, questions 61 and 62).
