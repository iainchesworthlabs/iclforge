**`forge qc`, `forge probe` and the Forge window's QC no longer fail Dolby's own TrueHD streams on the data rate.** The check simulated a queue at the declared peak rate and carried its backlog forward, so a long stream that is well within its rate (the four streams of Dolby Encoding Engine 6.5.4 with a tone in one channel at a time: 1.22 Mbit/s on average, 1.80 declared) showed 55,812 of 64,800 units late and exited 6. It now holds each unit to its own timing words: the unit before must have arrived before this one starts, a start follows the last by at least a quarter of a slot, and a unit must have arrived by its decode time with a lead of at least a slot and at most 75 ms (`libs/truehd/ERRATA.md`, entry 99; every bound is one the project's own streams sit inside). The `rate_model` member of the probe JSON loses `worst_latency_ms` and `latency_limit_ms` and gains `worst_overrun_ms`; `violations` counts the units that fail either check. The 27 hand-built streams of `testdata/truehd`, whose timing words mean nothing, now report violations.
