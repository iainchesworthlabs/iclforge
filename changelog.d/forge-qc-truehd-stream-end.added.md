**`forge qc` and the Forge window's QC check how a TrueHD or MLP stream ends.** The integrity gate now fails a terminator word (terminatorA with its `zero_samples` count, or `0xD234D234`) in an access unit that is not the last, anything but zero bits after the last block of the last unit that is not one of those words, and a `zero_samples` of 4096 or more; a stream with no word at its end passes, as before. These are the rules a licensed player was found to apply (TH spec section 16.5; `libs/truehd/ERRATA.md` entry 3). The decoder is unchanged and still takes all of them: they are counted, not refused. `forge probe detail=checks` prints the same counts as `stream end` and in `truehd.lossless.terminators`. The 147 streams of the DEE gold set all pass.
