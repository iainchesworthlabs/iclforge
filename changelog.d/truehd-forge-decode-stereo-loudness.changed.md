**`forge decode channels=2` is a TrueHD stream's own stereo presentation, and `forge loudness` says the dialnorm it implies.** `channels=2` reads the 2 channel substream the stream carries, as `truehd-presentation=2ch` does, instead of being refused; `loudness` of a TrueHD stream prints the dialnorm the loudness implies beside the one the stream stores for the presentation decoded.
