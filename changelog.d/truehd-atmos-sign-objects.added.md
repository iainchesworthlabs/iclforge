**`forge truehd-atmos sign-objects signing-key=PATH` signs the stream's evolution frames with the operator's key**, as E-AC-3's `sign-objects` does (the key may also come from `ICLFORGE_SIGNING_KEY[_FILE]`); without a key the stream is unsigned, as before. It is ICL Forge's own scheme, not a Dolby tag. The help and the command's status lines now say that the stream carries the Atmos flag, so a licensed decoder checks its frames and reports "Consecutive Evo frames fail security check" for tags that are not its own.
