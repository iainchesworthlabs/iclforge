**Crucible's TrueHD mode now holds the first 150 ms of audio back before it starts the device, so the stream no longer has gaps in it.** The encoder hands over a restart interval at once and then bursts in batches of five or six every third or fourth frame, and the device used to play from the first burst, so it waited on the next batch for the burst after: a 70 to 90 ms gap at the start and short gaps later, which a receiver reads as a lost lock. The leg now gathers at least 150 ms of bursts (`OutputStageConfig::truehd_prefill_ms`) and hands them to the sink together, on PipeWire, ALSA and WASAPI alike; the PCM and the other bitstream modes are unchanged. The cost is about 190 ms more start-up delay in this mode, paid once at each start of it. A new case plays the leg through a model of a device and fails without the pre-roll.
