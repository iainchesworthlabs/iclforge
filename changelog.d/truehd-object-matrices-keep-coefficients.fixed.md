**The object matrices of a TrueHD Atmos stream (restart sync 0x31EC) keep their coefficients between sections and move them by the extension's deltas as slopes; the lab tool is reduced to what the rule needs.** The coefficients of a matrix run at 2^-18 and keep their value when a section sends none; with the extension on, a delta is a change of `delta / 2^ext_field2` raw places in every access unit, applied as a ramp inside the unit (step 6552 a sample at 48 kHz) and added to the coefficient at the unit's end. The old reading (a constant refinement of 2^-(width + 1)) was wrong. The rule was found on the owner's controlled DEE encodes (a moving object over a smooth path, a jump, a fast move) and measured on 42 streams in a private copy of the decoder (106,308 of 106,308 substream 3 checks); the lab on this code passes every interval of the same 42 streams (106,308 of 106,308 checks). The hand-built extension stream and its Python reference are regenerated, and the kernel tests cover the ramp, its reset, the end-of-unit move, the bypass and the noise. `iclforge-truehd-ss3-lab` keeps the sections, sine and fit modes and loses its experiment switches. Provisional against outside decoders (ERRATA 69, `libs/truehd/SUBSTREAM3.md`).
