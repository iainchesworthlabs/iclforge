**The TrueHD encoder has the render side of an Atmos object stream.** `libs/truehd/src/encoder` gains `cluster_elements` (the master's objects, with the bed's channels pinned to their speakers, become the 12, 14 or 16 elements substream 3 carries: speaker-anchored elements 0 to 7, free elements 8 and up, merged by position and energy with a hysteresis, handed over without a step) and `render_bed` (a 7.1 panner held to `libs/render`'s, the bed-mix gains as a straight-line trajectory between section boundaries, and the primitive matrices that carry them). Internal, not yet reachable from `forge`: the stream writer takes the element signals today and keeps its fixed bed render until the matrix side adopts the plan. Provisional: DEE's own clustering is not known, so the rule is a documented guess (`libs/truehd/notes/feature-truehd-atmos-encoder-render.md`).
