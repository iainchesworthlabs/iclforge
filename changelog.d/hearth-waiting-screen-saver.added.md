**A screen with nothing playing now dims, shows the clock, or goes dark.** The status page a set-up Hearth player shows while it waits for something to play (its name, its address, "Waiting for something to play") was lit for hours. A new Preferences row, *When nothing is playing*, and `PUT /idle-view` choose what it does after the dim time: stay lit, dim (the default), show the clock, or go dark. A touch brings the status page back and the dim time begins again; a board still to be paired keeps the page lit; and a screen already dimmed as a song ends stays dimmed, so the page does not light between two songs.
