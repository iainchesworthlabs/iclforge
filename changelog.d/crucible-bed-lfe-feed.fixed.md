**Crucible's 5.1 bed now carries the LFE of the applications playing into it, so a subwoofer is no longer silent in DD+ 5.1, Atmos and TrueHD.** A surround application that rendered 5.1 or 7.1 into the bed had its LFE channel dropped by the bed mixer, and the engine never called the encoder's overload that takes an LFE, so the bed's LFE was empty whatever was playing. The mixer now sums the LFE channels of the bed applications and the engine hands that sum to the encoder beside its object slots, at unity. A positioned application's LFE is still dropped. A new case plays a 7.1 tap through the engine pinned to TrueHD, decodes the bursts, and finds the LFE channel carrying the tone; it fails without the change.
