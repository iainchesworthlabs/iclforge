**The Forge window's TrueHD and MLP player reads the stream through the cursor Hearth plays from.** Opening a file, a seek and an export use `TruehdCursor` and its index (`apps/shared/media/src/truehd_stream.hpp`) in place of the window's own scan, and the length is exact: the last unit's padding (TH spec section 16.5) is no longer counted (a 44.1 kHz stream of 11,025 samples showed 11,040). A seek decodes from the nearest of the restarts the index keeps, at most 256 units apart, so one into a stream with a restart at every unit costs a few more units' decode, 0.2 s at most.
