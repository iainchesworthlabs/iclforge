**The restart header writes the true `max_bits`, and `max_bits_repeat` the same.** The encoder wrote 24 in both, which is below the real bound for a sample of -2^23 (25 bits) and above it for every quieter interval; the Dolby decoder refuses a stream whose samples exceed `max_bits` (error `0xE7`). It is now the bit length of the largest output sample after the output shift, plus the sign, per restart interval and substream, and 0 for silence (TH spec §19.3).
