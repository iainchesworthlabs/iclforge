**`forge truehd-atmos` streams carry the Atmos flag `0x1000` in the major sync and the evolution frame of TH spec §15.4.1.** The encoder wrote flags 0, so the Dolby decoder never offered the 16-channel presentation of its streams; it now writes exactly `0x1000` for a stream with object channels. The evolution frame is the fixed TrueHD configuration (no group id, codec data `0x08`, frame aligned, one protection byte and no secondary) in place of the E-AC-3 container with 32 and 8 bit protection fields; the protection byte is zero, so the stream stays unsigned.
