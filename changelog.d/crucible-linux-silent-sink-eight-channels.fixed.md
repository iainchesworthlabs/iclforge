**On Linux, Crucible's silent output now says it is eight channels wide, so a 5.1 or 7.1 application reaches the bed by channel.** The silent sink was created with its eight speaker positions but no channel count, the sink enumeration reads the width from the count, and the engine therefore saw a device no channels wide and kept its taps at stereo: a surround application was folded to two channels before the bed mixer saw it, and its LFE never arrived. On a Raspberry Pi 4B a 5.1 file with a 60 Hz tone on the LFE alone reached the engine at -120 dBFS before, and arrives at full level through eight-channel taps with the count set.
