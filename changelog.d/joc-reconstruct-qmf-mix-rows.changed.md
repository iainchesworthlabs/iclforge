**Decoding Atmos objects (JOC in E-AC-3) is about 20 % faster, and the object reconstruction itself about a third.** Its mixing step, `reconstruct_qmf`, was 30 % of the decode: it decided the interpolation rule for every one of 320 coefficients a timeslot and object, with a table lookup and, for the ramps, a division each, all scalar. The rule is now picked once per object and timeslot and each rule is a loop of its own on the SIMD seam's two-lane double, with the rules that read only the band's coefficients worked out per parameter band, and the sum over the five bed channels runs two subbands a step. Every coefficient is the same IEEE operations in the same order (a division stays a division, no reciprocal, no fused multiply-add), and `f64x2` gained the one operator it needed, a division. MSVC Release on a Ryzen 9 5950X pinned to one logical processor, median of 7, two base/new pairs: `joc_reconstruct_qmf_4obj` 169,431/183,675 -> 120,284/117,413 ns (-29.0 %/-36.1 %), `joc_reconstruct_qmf_12obj` 403,716/474,569 -> 260,549/250,278 ns (-35.5 %/-47.3 %), `atmos_4obj_decode` 0.253/0.499 -> 0.203/0.201 ms a frame (-19.8 %/-59.7 %; the second base set was disturbed, its minimum 0.252 gives -22 %). The decoded WAVs and exported object WAVs of seven streams are byte-identical to the previous build's.
