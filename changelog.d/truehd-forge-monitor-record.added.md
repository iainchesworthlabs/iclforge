**`forge monitor` plays a TrueHD or MLP stream, and `forge record ... codec=truehd` takes a lossless one.** `monitor` decodes the presentation `truehd-presentation=` names to the output as the units decode, asking the device for the stream's own channel count and rate (a shared-mode device that does not take them says so, exit 4). `record` holds a take of one to eight channels as PCM and writes it as a bare `.thd`; `live` does not code TrueHD and says so.
