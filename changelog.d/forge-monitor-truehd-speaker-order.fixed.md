**`forge monitor` plays a 7.1 TrueHD stream's side and rear surrounds on the right speakers.** The output was opened with no speaker mask, so the 7.1 mask's own order (rears before sides) was applied to a stream that lists its sides first, and the two pairs swapped places. `monitor` now opens the output with the mask of the stream's channels and puts them in that mask's order (a stream with a channel no speaker bit names, such as the second LFE, is still sent in its own order, and the run says so). Eight channels at 96 kHz and six at 192 kHz, the most TrueHD carries there, play through the default output.
