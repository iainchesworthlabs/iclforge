**`forge truehd-atmos` and `EncoderConfig::object_channels` write a TrueHD Atmos stream (WP1 step 4; provisional).** The channels of a WAV (the first the LFE), or of an ADM master (its LFE first, then its beds and objects on the paths it gives them; ADM builds), are substream 3, with restart sync 0x31EC and the 16-channel extension in the major sync (32 bytes, as the owner's discs have it); the 7.1 bed is in substreams 0 to 2, from a WAV (`truehd-bed=`) or the objects placed on 7.1 by `libs/render` from the metadata the stream carries; the objects' positions (a scene file as `atmos-path` takes it, or an even fan) are in the evolution frames, one every 1536 samples. The bed's substreams are the 7.1 stream of the bed alone, byte for byte: FFmpeg's strict decode, which leaves substream 3 alone, returns the bed; `forge decode` returns the objects bit for bit and the best presentation equals its own render of the objects (`truehd-render=`). Held by `test_encoder_atmos.cpp`, `test_cli_truehd_objects.cpp`, the manifest outcome `bed` of the hand-built streams and `tools/checks/verify_truehd_atmos_interop.py` (CI). Unsigned; nothing outside this repository reads substream 3 (`libs/truehd/ERRATA.md` entries 63 and 64, question 63).
