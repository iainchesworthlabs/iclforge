**The lossless check of a TrueHD substream with 8 or more matrix channels shifts a sample by the channel number modulo 8.** The plain shift pushed the top bits of a 24-bit sample out of the 32-bit word from channel 8 up, so that the object substream of a Dolby Atmos stream failed its checks whenever a negative sample sat in an upper channel. The decoder, the encoder and the Python reference now agree; the hand-built 16-channel streams are regenerated.
