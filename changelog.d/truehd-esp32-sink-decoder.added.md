**The Hearth sink decodes TrueHD, and the ESP32-P4 has played it.** `CONFIG_ICLFORGE_TRUEHD` builds `libs/truehd`'s decoder (a decode-only archive, `libs/truehd/minimal.cmake`) into the ESP-IDF component; the burst player decodes the Sendspin `truehd` data type (one to 24 whole access units a chunk) and plays a unit it cannot read as silence. A board lists `truehd` with its own channel limit (`CONFIG_ICLFORGE_EXAMPLE_SENDSPIN_TRUEHD*`; the P4 states 6), and its decode task has 72 KiB of stack with `sdkconfig.truehd`. On an ESP32-P4 the repository's test server played a 2.0 and a 5.1 fixture over Wi-Fi without a late, dropped or invalid chunk, at 0.18 and 0.40 of a core. **Not done:** the S3 and C6 in a sink, a listen on a speaker, and a now-view name for TrueHD.
