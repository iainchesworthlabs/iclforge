**The 7.1 bed of a TrueHD Atmos stream is rendered by the law measured on the owner's DEE controls.** `iclforge/truehd/encoder/bed_pan.hpp` gives an object's 6 bit place (x 0 to 62, y 0 to 62, z 0 to 15) its shares of the bed's rows: cosine and sine splits between the front, side and rear rows and then along each row, the outer product between rows, no object-count headroom, the layout's trim `T * w` (-1.5 dB for 7.1, `w = min(1, y / 37.2 + max(0, (z / 15 - 0.2) / 0.8))`) as the only fixed gain, and a limiter at 0 dBFS with 32 samples of look-ahead for a sum over the ceiling. `forge truehd-atmos` renders its bed with it (the encoder's element clustering uses it as the default pan law, and `forge decode truehd-render=` of the 7.1 layout shows the same bed) in place of the layout renderer and a hard clip. Held to the owner's controls: a lone object at a fixed place is within 0.02 % of the tone, and along a wall the holds sit on the law's values (median error 2e-4 to 5e-4 of the gain); the moves between places follow the metadata's one-frame ramp, where the DEE controls' own beds ramp over 1.6 k or 5.1 k samples. Interior places, height and everything of size, zone and snap are not measured (`libs/truehd/notes/feature-truehd-measured-bed-render.md`). Provisional like the rest of substream 3.
