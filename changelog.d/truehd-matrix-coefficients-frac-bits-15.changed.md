**The TrueHD decoder reads matrix coefficients to `frac_bits` 15 (WP1 step 1, in part).** Coefficients are scaled to 2^-15 and the sum shifted by 15; the output of a stream with `frac_bits` of 14 or less is the same sample for sample. The owner's discs' 12-channel substream does not decode yet: whether its block header has other fields is open (ERRATA question 60), and `tools/checks/truehd_substream3_search.py` is the check the owner runs on a disc excerpt (it prints parameters and counts only). The Python reference reads the 32-byte major sync.
