**The TrueHD encoder takes a general downmix of the objects into the 7.1 bed (`EncoderConfig::object_downmix`), with four to seven bed rows that are one scaled object closed in at most 15 primitive matrices, and a bed row of zeros set by the lower substreams.** It is an 8 by N matrix of gains (`ObjectLayout::kBedMatrices`, 9 to 16 objects) in place of the fixed render. Substream 3's chain follows TH spec 10.2.1d: each bed row takes the object of its largest gain through a non-identity `ch_assign`, a tuning primitive makes the row's pivot 1 and a main primitive makes it the downmix row; a row that is one scaled object gets an overlap primitive on the spare coded row and, with two or more of them, one shared primitive on the spare tunes them together (15 primitives for four to seven such rows, where the first design needed 18 to 21). A row that is exactly zero cannot be made in substream 3, so the chain leaves the object in it and a matrix with no coefficients in each lower substream sets it to 0. The streams decode losslessly with the library's strict decoder (objects back sample for sample, every substream's lossless check passing) and the bed is within -87 dB of the downmix on the test streams; eight single-object rows (17 primitives), a row that is a combination of the other rows, a coefficient of 32 or more and audio whose bed or coded rows pass 24 bits are refused by name. The downmix is static (a moving one needs the slopes of the object matrices), and the accuracy of rows of several objects (pair rows, pan laws of 9 and 10 objects) is the specification's open question: they decode losslessly, their bed is not claimed. No decoder outside this repository reads restart sync 0x31EC; the Dolby reference player has not read these streams.
