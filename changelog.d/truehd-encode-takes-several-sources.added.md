**`forge truehd-encode` takes several WAVs and places each channel by name.** `src=`, `map=` and `offset=` work as they do for the other encoders, with TrueHD's channel names for the places: `map=0.0:L,0.1:R,1.0:C,2.0:LFE,2.1:Ls,2.2:Rs` puts the two channels of the input and the first channel of each of two `src=` files into 5.1 (`L R C LFE Ls Rs Lvh Rvh Lc Rc Lrs Rrs Cs Ts Lsd Rsd Lw Rw Cvh LFE2`, any case). The places must make one channel assignment, and the stream's channels are in that assignment's order whatever the order of the map; a set that is not one is refused with the sets nearest to it. A place named twice, a source or channel that is not there, a source with no channel in the map, sources of two sample rates and `src=` with no `map=` are refused and nothing is written. `offset=<source>:<seconds>` puts silence ahead of a source, and a shorter source is padded with silence. A Forge project of codec `truehd` with several sources, `assignments` rows (`to` a TrueHD channel name) and `offsetSeconds` becomes the same command. `planning/truehd-parity-forge.md`, question 16.
