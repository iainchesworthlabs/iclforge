**`forge project` runs a project of codec `truehd`.** One WAV in `sources` and `encode.truehd.verify` and `encode.truehd.downmix` become `forge truehd-encode` and its two options, with a container wrapped by `mkv`, `mp4`, `ts` or `spdif` like any other codec's; what a lossless encode does not have (several sources, assignments, Atmos, fragmented MP4, the AC-3 family's metadata options) is refused with a sentence, not run with it dropped.
