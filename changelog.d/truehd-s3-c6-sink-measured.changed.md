**TrueHD in the S3 and C6 sinks was played and measured.** The ESP32-S3 played a 2.0 stream over Sendspin without a late, dropped or invalid chunk (31,200 of 31,200 in a ten-minute play, 0 underruns), with a burst of 0.83 of the 20 ms chunk, over the 0.7 the plan asks for; so it does not list `truehd` by default and takes it by hand with `CONFIG_ICLFORGE_EXAMPLE_SENDSPIN_TRUEHD=1`. The ESP32-C6 cannot: it has 53 KB of internal RAM free after start-up and no PSRAM against a 158 KB decoder and a 72 KiB stack, and on the 4 MB profile the image is 37 KB over its slot. The planning page records both, with the figures. The ESP32-P4 played a 7.1 stream of about 5 Mbit/s cleanly, and failed a 7.6 Mbit/s noise stream on the link, not the decoder. **Not done:** a listen on a speaker and a bit-exact capture.
